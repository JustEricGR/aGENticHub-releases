aGENtic Hub

Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

aGENtic Hub is a desktop application for Windows. It runs on your own PC. The developer runs no servers for it and receives none of your data: there is no account with us, no analytics and no telemetry.

Signing in with Google

When you choose "Sign in with Google", the app asks Google for your basic profile: your name, email address and profile picture (the openid, email and profile scopes). They are used only to show, inside the app, which Google account is signed in.

Google gives the app a token, which is stored in Windows Credential Manager on your PC. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

If you turn on a Google tool in the app (for example Google Drive, Gmail or Calendar), the app asks Google for that service's access at that moment, and Google shows you what is requested. The app then reads or writes in that service only when an agent works on a task you asked for, directly from your PC to Google.

Signing in with GitHub

When you choose "Sign in with GitHub", GitHub gives the app a token with access to your repositories (the repo and read:org scopes). It is stored in Windows Credential Manager and used only to create, update and share the repositories that hold your projects, in your own GitHub account. New repositories are private unless you choose otherwise.

The AI models

To work on a task, the app sends what that task needs to the AI model providers you configured in the app (for example your Databricks workspace, Google AI Studio or OpenRouter). That can include text from files you attach and, if you turned on a Google tool, content the agent read from that service for the task. What those providers do with it is governed by their own terms.

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the features you use in the app. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else, and it is not used to train AI models by the developer. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Updates

When the app starts, it checks GitHub for a newer version and downloads it from this site's GitHub repository. As with any download, GitHub receives your IP address.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in Settings → Accounts. Signing out of Google also revokes the app's access at Google.
  • You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and github.com/settings/applications.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting %LOCALAPPDATA%\aGENticHub removes everything the app stored on your PC. Repositories in your GitHub account stay there until you delete them.
